Community Guidelines

Last updated: July 2026

LifeMaxxing is for real experiences in the real world. Be decent. Don’t use the map to harass people.

Do

  • Log genuine experiences (activity + place/pin + date).
  • Respect others’ privacy and visibility choices.
  • Use report and block tools when something feels wrong.

Don’t

  • Harassment, hate speech, threats, or bullying.
  • Illegal content, scams, spam, or impersonation.
  • Sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance).
  • Doxxing or sharing someone’s private home / exact private location without consent.
  • Scraping, fake engagement, or abuse of the platform.

Enforcement

We may remove content, mute visibility, or suspend accounts. Moderators aim to review reports within 24 hours. Appeals: support@lifemaxxing.com.
