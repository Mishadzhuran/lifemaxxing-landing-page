Privacy Policy

Aligned with the LifeMaxxing app · Last updated July 24, 2026

This page covers founding-member registration on the marketing site. The full product Privacy Policy (accounts, location, photos, UGC, deletion) is the same one used in the app: lifemaxxing.io/legal/privacy/

What we collect on this site

If you register as a founding member, we collect your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you about early access and app download availability.

How we use it

  • To send early-access and launch notifications
  • To confirm your place on the founding-member list
  • To improve LifeMaxxing based on aggregate interest

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use founding-member contact details for ads tracking. We only contact you about early access and product updates related to LifeMaxxing.

App data (once you use LifeMaxxing)

When you create an account in the app, we may also process account details, experience content, location, photos, identifiers, and diagnostics as described in the full Privacy Policy. Minimum age is 16 (or higher if required where you live).

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your founding-member registration, and you can delete an app account at lifemaxxing.io/account/delete/.
