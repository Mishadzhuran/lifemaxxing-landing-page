How it works

Three steps to a life on record.

Discover what is next, log what mattered, and watch your world fill in on the map — honestly, without the noise.

Step 01

Discover places worth your time.

Browse experiences near you or further afield. Filter by category, distance and rating — and lean on recommendations from people you trust.

Step 02

Log the moment while it is fresh.

Capture the place, rating, photos and a short story in seconds. Good moments deserve more than a camera roll.

Step 03

See your world on the Life Map.

Every log drops a pin. Zoom out and your life becomes a map — from the corner café to cities you return to.

Questions

Is LifeMaxxing free?

Yes. LifeMaxxing is free to download on iOS and Android. Any paid features, if introduced, will be described clearly in the app and store listings.

Do I need an account?

You create an account to save logs, build your map and connect with friends. You can delete your account at any time from the account deletion page.

What can I log?

Places and experiences across dining, adventure, wellness, travel and more — with ratings, photos and notes so the moment stays findable later.

Where do recommendations come from?

From people whose taste you trust, plus ratings and discovery tools in the app. We do not invent fake social proof on this site.

Need help with your account?

Visit Support or email hello@lifemaxxing.com. For account removal, use Delete account.
