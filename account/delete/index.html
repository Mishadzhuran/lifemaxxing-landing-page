Account deletion

Required for Google Play when the app offers accounts.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open LifeMaxxing and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your profile is soft-deleted and associated content is scheduled for removal.

Delete by email

Email support@lifemaxxing.com from the address on your account. Include your username if you know it. We process requests within a reasonable period (typically within 30 days).

What is deleted

Profile, experiences you authored, media you uploaded, follows, and related personal data we no longer need to keep for legal or security reasons. Some anonymized or aggregated analytics may remain. Content others created that mentions you (e.g. comments) may remain with identifiers removed where feasible.
